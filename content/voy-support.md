# Voy Support

Help with Voy, the podcast player for iPhone that skips ads for you. Find an answer below, or write to us. We read every message.

## Contact us

Email: **dreamatmai@gmail.com**

Had a bad skip, or an ad that played through? In Voy, use **Report a Problem**. It opens an email with the episode and the ad times already filled in, so we can fix it fast. You see the email before you send it.

## Ad skipping

**How does Voy skip ads?**
Our servers check each episode ahead of time and mark where its ads are. When you play the episode, your iPhone matches the audio against that data and jumps past each ad on its own. There is nothing to tap.

**Does Voy use my microphone?**
No. Voy never listens to your surroundings. It only checks the episode audio it is already playing, on your iPhone. No audio leaves your device.

**An ad played, or Voy skipped part of the show.**
Some shows insert different ads for different listeners, and new episodes can take a short time to be checked. If a skip was wrong, please use **Report a Problem** while that episode is open. The report tells us exactly where it happened.

**Why can't I find a show?**
Voy carries a catalog of shows we can check for ads, and it grows over time. To ask for a show, email us its name.

## Subscription

**Is Voy free?**
You get 5 free ad skips to try it. After that, a Voy subscription keeps ads skipped on every episode. Plans, prices and any free trial show in the app before you buy.

**How do I cancel?**
Apple handles billing. On your iPhone, open **Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Voy** and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the period ends.

**I paid, but Voy asks me to subscribe again.**

- Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
- Tap **Restore Purchases** on the subscription screen.
- If that does not help, email us with the date of purchase. Do not send card details.

**How do I get a refund?**
Apple processes refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Your listening

**Does my progress sync to my other devices?**
Yes, if you are signed in to iCloud. Voy keeps your history and playback positions in your private iCloud, so they follow you. We cannot read that data. To turn it off, go to **Settings > your name > iCloud**.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Voy has no sign-up and asks for no name or email.

**What data does Voy collect?**
As little as we can. The details are in our [Privacy Policy](/voy/privacy/). Use of Voy is covered by our [Terms of Service](/voy/terms/).
