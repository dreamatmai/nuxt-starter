Voi is a simple podcast player for your Mac. Find an answer below, or write to us. We read every message.

Is something wrong with an episode? In Voi, open **Voi > About Voi** and choose **Report a Problem…**. It opens an email with the episode already filled in. You see the email before you send it.

## About Voi

One window holds everything: your categories and recent shows on the left, the shows in the middle, the player on the right. There is no library to manage, no feed to set up and no account to make. Pick a show, press Play and listen.

![The Voi window: the News shows, the sidebar and the player](/voi/01-player.jpg)

The catalog is curated. Voi brings you hundreds of the most popular shows, sorted into categories such as News, Comedy, True Crime, Business, Sports and History, with the newest episodes first.

Open a show to see its episodes. Each episode picks up where you stopped.

## Questions

**How do I find a show?**
Choose a category in the sidebar, or type a name in **Find a show** at the top of the window. To ask for a show that Voi does not carry, email us its name.

**Which keys control playback?**
Space plays and pauses. Command-Left Arrow goes back 15 seconds and Command-Right Arrow goes forward 30 seconds. The media keys and Control Center work too. The **Controls** menu lists them all.

**I closed the window. How do I get it back?**
The audio goes on when the window is closed. Click Voi's icon in the Dock, or choose **Window > Voi**.

**Is Voi free?**
Yes. Voi has no subscription and no in-app purchase.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Voi has no sign-up and asks for no name or email.

**How does Voi skip ads?**
Our server marks where the ads are in each episode. While an episode plays, Voi compares the audio it downloads with those marks and moves past each ad. There is nothing to tap.

**Does Voi use my microphone?**
No. Voi never uses the microphone, and no audio leaves your Mac.

**An ad played, or Voi skipped part of the show.**
Some shows insert different ads for different listeners, and a new episode can take a short time to be checked. Please use **Report a Problem…** while that episode is open, so we can see where it happened.

**What data does Voi collect?**
None. The details are in our [Privacy Policy](/voi/privacy/). Use of Voi is covered by our [Terms of Service](/voi/terms/).

**What does Voi need?**
A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.
